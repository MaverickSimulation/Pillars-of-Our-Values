How We Work Around Here

This isn't a list of rules. It's a description of the people we are and the standards we hold ourselves to — because we believe this is how great work actually gets done.

We hired you because we believe you're excellent. Since continued excellence needs a framework. These are ours — built to give it the right shape.

The Seven Pillars

01 Own It

You put your name on your work. You flag problems early. You don't wait to be chased, and you don't pass the problem sideways. We love to see you proactive and on top of things.

02 Deliver Reliably

Deadlines are commitments, not for tomorrow. If something changes, you communicate it first — not after the deadline passes.

03 Make Others Better

Your knowledge doesn't stay with you. You share, teach, and unblock the people around you. A rising tide lifts everyone. We love to see you shine and mentor.

04 Work Securely

Company information stays on company-approved tools. No exceptions. This protects our clients, our colleagues, and the firm.

05 Grow Deliberately

You are expected to be better at your job every six months than you were before. Stagnation is not neutral — it is a choice we don't make here.

06 Collaborate Without Politics

Credit belongs to the work, not the loudest voice in the room. We recognise contribution, not performance. There is no audience to play to here.

07 Set Goals. Hit Them.

Every quarter you set goals with your manager. Every quarter you are measured against them. Ambiguity about what is expected is not acceptable — from you or your manager.

What This Looks Like in Practice

Not This This
Waiting to be asked for an update Proactively flagging progress and blockers
Sharing work files on WhatsApp Using only approved communication tools
Solving a problem and moving on Documenting the solution so others benefit
Being secretive to get ahead Delivering consistently to get ahead
Surfacing a problem without a view on the fix Surfacing a problem with a proposed solution
